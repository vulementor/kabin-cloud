# Governance is an execution boundary, not a warning banner.

Approvals and policy must control actual external effects.

## Responsibility

1. Risk and permission policy
2. Human approval gate
3. Immutable operation evidence

## Example

A routine can prepare a public post, but the authorized user must approve the publication action.
