# Creative & Media Capacity

Specialized video, audio and visual operations that solutions can compose.

## Contract overview

- Input: Brief / image / audio
- Output: Frames, voice, video or captions
- Validation: Format, source version and media probe

## Scope

A Capacity should say exactly whether it generates images, narrates, edits or renders. One label cannot substitute for the missing step.

## Example

A short-video recipe may use separate voice, scene detection and rendering capacities.
