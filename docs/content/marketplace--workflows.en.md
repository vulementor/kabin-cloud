# Workflow & Routine Capacity

Predictable tasks with checkpoints, durable artifacts and safe recovery.

## Contract overview

- Input: Approved trigger + inputs
- Output: Repeatable job + artifact
- Validation: Idempotency, checkpoints and receipts

## Scope

A Routine runs a known sequence. Retrying after an unknown external effect requires reconciliation.

## Example

A weekly market brief can use a Routine for source checks, formatting and draft assembly.
