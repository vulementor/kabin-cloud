WHY KABIN

Not another chatbot. An operating layer for work.

Answers alone are not outcomes. Real work moves across tools, systems, approvals and handoffs. Kabin is designed to connect these pieces while keeping the human in control.

THE HYBRID MODEL

One intent. Many capabilities. A single coordinated flow.

Use reasoning where judgment matters, routines where repeatability matters, and human approval wherever authority matters.

Conceptual flow. Specific product behavior depends on integrations and deployment.
◎STEP 01 / 04

Define the outcome

State what needs to be achieved, not every click needed to get there.

✳STEP 02 / 04

Plan the route

An agent selects the tools, routines and capabilities relevant to the goal.

↻STEP 03 / 04

Execute and validate

Routines and connected systems perform the work, with checks along the way.

◇STEP 04 / 04

Retain the context

Outcomes, artifacts and decisions inform the next step.
